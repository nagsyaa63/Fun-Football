# ⚽ Fun Football — Player Guide

Welcome to **Fun Football**, a free, for-fun prediction game for the 2026 FIFA
World Cup. Predict matches, earn points for bold correct calls, climb the
leaderboards, build a friends league, and earn football titles — all just for
bragging rights.

> 💛 This is **not** gambling. No money is staked or exchanged — it's purely for
> fun and friendly competition among football fans.

---

## 1. What is Fun Football?

For every World Cup match you make **two predictions**:

1. 🏆 **The result** — Home win, Draw, or Away win.
2. ⭐ **The MVP** — the Man of the Match (any player from either team).

You score points when you're right. The clever twist: the **fewer** people who
backed the correct result, the **more** points you earn — so backing a brave
underdog pick is worth far more than always picking the favourite.

---

## 2. Getting started

1. Click **Log in / Sign up** in the top-right.
2. Choose a **username** and a **password** (at least 6 characters).
3. That's it — you're in! You get a unique **player ID** (e.g. `U7F3K9Q`) that
   tracks your points across every device.
4. Use the same username and password to log back in anywhere; your points,
   title and history stay with you.

No email, no verification, no third-party login. Simple and quick.

---

## 3. Making predictions

- Open matches appear under the **🟢 Open** section.
- For each match, pick the **result** and the **MVP**, then **Save prediction**.
- You can **change your prediction as often as you like** — right up until the
  match locks. A small "✓ saved" note confirms your pick is in.
- Predict as many or as few matches as you like.

---

## 4. When predictions lock 🔒

- Each match **locks** shortly before kickoff (a couple of minutes by default).
- Once locked you **cannot add or change** your prediction for that match.
- A live **countdown** shows when it locks — it turns **amber** under an hour and
  **red** in the final minutes, so you don't miss it.

Matches are grouped so the important ones are easy to find:

- **🟢 Open** — predict now (soonest to lock first).
- **🔒 Locked** — picks are in, awaiting the result.
- **✅ Finished** — the result and points are in.

---

## 5. See how the crowd voted 📊

As soon as a match **locks**, each card reveals what everyone predicted — the
percentage who chose Home / Draw / Away. (Hidden while open so it can't sway your
pick.) Great for spotting which results the crowd thinks are "obvious" — and
where the big points are hiding.

---

## 6. Scoring — the "Giant Slayer" system 🗡️

Tap **❓ Scoring** any time for this in the app. Result points scale with how
popular the correct result was:

- Only ~**10%** backed the actual result → about **9 points**.
- A **50/50** split → **5 points**.
- ~**90%** backed it (the obvious pick) → about **1 point**.

In short: `points = (1 − share who picked it) × 10`, minimum 1 point for any
correct call. Bold, correct underdog picks are the big earners.

**MVP points:** a flat **+5** for correctly naming the Man of the Match.

**Overall points = Giant Slayer (result) points + MVP points.**

After every finished match you'll see a breakdown like *"Only 18% backed Japan →
correct picks earned +8"* — and **how many points you personally earned**.

## 7. Titles 🏅

As your total points grow, you climb football-themed titles:

`🆕 Debutant → 🧤 Squad Player → 👕 First XI → 🎯 Playmaker → 🔥 Star Striker →
🅒 Captain → 🏅 Maestro → 👑 Legend → 🐐 GOAT`

Your current title and progress to the next show in your stats bar.

---

## 8. Leaderboards 🏆

Four leaderboards, always available:

- **Overall** — total points (Giant Slayer + MVP).
- **🗡️ Giant Slayer** — points from bold, correct result calls.
- **⭐ MVP** — points from correct MVP picks.
- **📊 Average** — average points per match (rewards consistency). You need a
  minimum number of predicted matches (default 5) to appear here.

Notes:

- Each board shows the **top 100** players.
- Even if you're outside the top 100, you'll see **your own rank and points**
  below the table, with a **YOU** tag on your row.
- A **"Last updated"** time is shown in **IST and UTC**.

## 9. Friend leagues 🏟️

Want to compete with just your mates or office?

1. Click **🏟️ Leagues** → **Create** a league (give it a name).
2. You'll get a short **invite code** — tap **Share** to send it to friends.
3. Friends click **🏟️ Leagues** → enter the code → **Join**.
4. On the leaderboard, use the **"Showing"** selector to switch between 🌍 Global
   and any of your leagues.

Everyone keeps their normal global points — leagues are just a filtered view.

---

## 10. Your profile 👤

Open **Profile** (top-right, once logged in) to:

- Change your **username**.
- Set the **team you support** (optional) — shows as a flag by your name.
- Add your **Instagram** (optional).

### 🔒 About your Instagram (privacy)

Your Instagram is **private** — it is **never shown to other players**. It's
visible **only to the organisers**, purely so they can reach out if you win any
post-tournament goodies. You can leave it blank.

---

## 11. Share your picks 📲

- On a **locked** match, share your locked-in pick ("I'm backing X").
- On a **finished** match you got right, share how many points you earned and how
  few people agreed with you.
- Sharing uses your device's normal share sheet (or copies text to paste).

## 14. Fair play & responsible fun

- **No money, no betting.** Fun Football is free and for entertainment only.
- Any post-tournament giveaways (if offered) are goodwill gestures — **not** tied
  to wagering and **not** guaranteed by your rank.
- Play fair: one account per person. Respect fellow fans!

---

## 15. Quick tips to climb

- **Be brave** — correctly backing an upset few others saw is where the big
  Giant Slayer points are.
- **Don't skip the MVP** — a flat +5 every match adds up fast.
- **Predict early, edit later** — lock in a pick, then refine before kickoff.
- **Watch the countdown colours** — amber = under an hour, red = last minutes.
- **Play consistently** to climb the Average board, not just Overall.

---

## 16. FAQ

**Do I need to predict every match?** No — predict whichever you like.

**Can I change a prediction?** Yes, any time until the match locks.

**Forgot my password?** There's no reset yet, so keep it safe. (Don't reuse an
important password — this is a fun, low-stakes app.)

**Why only 2 points for a correct pick?** Most people picked the same result —
obvious calls are worth less. Back the underdog for more!

**Why can't I see others' Instagram?** It's private by design — organisers only.

**Is my data sold?** No. It's a small fun project, not an ad network.
