🗡️ How scoring works

For each match you predict two things:

  • The result — Home win, Draw, or Away win.
  • The MVP — any player from either team.

🗡️ Giant Slayer points reward bold correct calls. The fewer people who backed the actual result, the more you earn:

  • Only ~10% picked it → about 9 pts
  • 50/50 split → 5 pts
  • ~90% picked it (the obvious pick) → about 1 pt

⭐ MVP = a flat bonus for the correct player. Overall = Giant Slayer + MVP.

So backing an underdog that comes through is worth far more than always picking the favourite. After each match you'll see exactly what you earned and what % of players agreed with you.
